Your Google Business Profile can be one of the first things a local customer sees when searching for your business or services.

But simply creating a profile is not enough.

Incorrect categories, missing services, outdated hours, weak photos, inconsistent business information or poor alignment with your website can make the profile less useful to both customers and Google.

Goodfaith’s Google Business Profile Optimization services improve how accurately and clearly your business is represented across Google Search and Maps.

The goal is not to fill every available field.

It is to create a profile that clearly explains what your business does, where it operates and why a customer should choose it.

## What Is Google Business Profile Optimization?

Google Business Profile Optimization is the process of improving the information, services, categories, images, reviews and other elements of your profile so customers and Google can better understand your business.

Optimization can include:

- Primary and additional categories
- Services
- Business description
- Address or service areas
- Hours
- Phone and website information
- Business attributes
- Photos and videos
- Posts and updates
- Review strategy
- Profile links
- Website alignment
- Performance tracking

Google recommends keeping Business Profile information complete and accurate and specifically highlights categories, hours, reviews, photos and other business details as useful parts of local visibility.

## Why Isn't My Google Business Profile Performing Better?

A profile may be verified and active but still have problems.

Common issues include:

- The wrong primary category
- Too many unrelated additional categories
- Important services missing
- Services on GBP not matching the website
- Outdated business hours
- Incorrect service areas
- Weak or generic business descriptions
- Very few real business photos
- Reviews not being monitored
- Business details that differ across the web
- A website that does not support the same services
- No clear measurement of calls, clicks or customer activity

Goodfaith starts by finding the gaps instead of making random profile changes.

## GBP Category & Service Optimization

Categories help Google and customers understand what type of business you operate.

Google specifically states that selected categories affect local ranking and recommends choosing the most specific primary category that accurately describes the business.

Goodfaith reviews:

- Primary category
- Relevant additional categories
- Available services
- Custom services where appropriate
- Service descriptions
- Relationship between categories and actual business offerings

Google also allows eligible service businesses to list services, organize them by category and add details such as descriptions or prices. Those services may be shown when customers search for them.

We do not add categories simply because competitors use them. They should accurately represent what the business actually does.

## Website & Google Business Profile Alignment

If your profile says your main service is one thing while your website emphasizes something completely different, the overall business story becomes less clear.

## Reviews & Local Trust

Reviews can influence how customers compare local businesses.

Google says responding to reviews shows that a business values customer feedback, and more reviews and positive ratings can contribute to local prominence.

Goodfaith can help create a practical review process around:

- Requesting genuine customer reviews
- Making review links easy to access
- Monitoring new reviews
- Writing appropriate responses
- Identifying recurring customer concerns
- Avoiding fake or manipulated reviews

The objective is genuine reputation—not manufactured ratings.
