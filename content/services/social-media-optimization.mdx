## What Is Social Media Optimization?

Social Media Optimization (SMO) improves how a business presents itself, publishes content and engages with audiences across social platforms.

It can include:

- Profile optimization
- Content strategy
- Educational posts
- Short-form videos
- YouTube content
- Creative editing
- Brand consistency
- Captions and messaging
- Audience engagement
- Content planning
- Performance review

The right strategy depends on what your customers actually want to watch, learn, research and interact with.

Good social media should provide value before asking for attention.
