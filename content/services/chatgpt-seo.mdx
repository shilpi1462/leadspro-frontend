## Can My Website Actually Appear in ChatGPT Search?

Yes.

OpenAI states that public websites can appear in ChatGPT Search. To help content become eligible to be discovered, surfaced and clearly cited, OpenAI recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot to access the website. Your hosting provider, firewall or CDN may also need to permit OpenAI’s published search-crawler traffic.

Allowing OAI-SearchBot does not guarantee visibility or placement. OpenAI says ChatGPT Search uses multiple factors intended to surface relevant and reliable information.

OpenAI also distinguishes between:

### OAI-SearchBot

Used for search discovery and helping content appear in ChatGPT Search experiences.

### GPTBot

Related to content that may be used to improve OpenAI’s generative AI models.

Website owners can manage these separately.

## How Do We Measure ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO should be measured through observable evidence — not an invented “ChatGPT ranking score.”

Depending on what can reliably be measured, we may examine:

- **Brand Mentions** — Does the business appear for relevant questions?
- **Website Citations** — Is ChatGPT citing or linking to the business’s website?
- **Pages Referenced** — Which pages are being discovered?
- **Important Question Visibility** — Does the business appear when customers ask commercially relevant questions?
- **ChatGPT Referral Traffic** — Are people clicking through from ChatGPT to the website?
- **Business Outcomes** — Are AI-referred visitors generating enquiries, calls or other meaningful actions?

OpenAI states that publishers allowing OAI-SearchBot can track ChatGPT referral traffic using analytics platforms. ChatGPT referral URLs automatically include utm_source=chatgpt.com.

### Consistent Testing Matters

</Flow>

Changing the questions repeatedly until your company appears does not create meaningful measurement.
